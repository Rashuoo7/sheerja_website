Who We Are

Dedicated To Excellence

Shreeja Marine Services was incorporated on 27 Sept 2016. We’re India’s leading provider of ship & crew management services. Driven by passion and a strong work ethic, we pride ourselves in providing complete end-to-end shipping solutions to ship owners and companies, and helping young aspirants start a successful career in the merchant navy.

From Crew Management and Technical Management to Flag Documentation and E-Migration services for seafarers, Shreeja Marine Services has built a reputation for reliability, efficiency, and customer-centric service. The company caters to a wide range of industries, including oil & gas, manufacturing, retail, automotive, and bulk commodities.
